Use "~" or "`" to open and close the command console
WASD to move
Space to jump or fly up
Left shift to sprint or fly down

(also, in console scroll down to go up and up to go down)

Commands needed-

Network.Host()
- Hosts and starts a game

Network.Connect()
- Connect to a host. In parenthesis, put 8 letter join code displayed in the console by host. No need for quotation marks.

Player.Gamemode()
- Changes game mode. In parenthesis, put 0 for default and 1 for spectator
- HINT: If you are spawned outside of map, (can happen), do Player.Gamemode(1) to fly back up into map

Buildsystem.Building()
-Activates or deactivates build system. Put "true" or "false" to activate or deactivate (no quotation marks) in parenthesis.

Buildsystem.BuildId()
- Changes the object you are building. Put 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4 into parenthesis.
- 0 gives you a foundation (default), 1 gives you a ceiling, 2 gives you a ramp, 3 gives you walls, and 4 is a cube thing

Help()
- Displays a list of commands and what they do. If you want to try playing around with them feel free but have fun
