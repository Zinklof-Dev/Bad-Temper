<h3>
    General Idea
</h3>

Endless wave defense fps. Utilizing a grid based building system, a simplistic but long progression system, and medieval fantasy era weapons and the such, players defend against hordes of enemies that get increasingly larger and introduce tougher and tougher enemies.

<h3>
    Game Type
</h3>

Reliant on Netcode, and Relay for over the network sessions, and taking inspiration from rougelites by having no save system and reseting if the players fail to defend their objective. and allowing enemies to drop certain "legendary" items for players to use and help them progress.

<h3>
    Enemy System
</h3>

Idealy 8-16 different basic enemies, though thats a little ambitous at 16, and 3-5 bosses to keep things fresh. The waves would operate on a day night cycle, where the players get 2 or so minutes to prepare during the day, for the night where they must survive the entire wave before progressing back to day time.

<h3>
    Day / Night Cycle
</h3>

Players would scavenge for resources during the day to build their bases, make new weapons armor and workstations to progress down the tech tree. at night players would be punished for attempting to gather resources not by a hand wave but more so of the threat of enemies and lack of base defense while they are gone.

<h3>
    Campfire Structure
</h3>

Enemies are/would be attempting to attack and destroy a centeral structure, players cannot repair this structure but it will slowly heal over time.

<h3>
    Combat
</h3>

Players would have to spec into different paths, from fighting with melee, being a ranged archer, healing, combat magic, etc etc. This would support play styles and help make each player have more of a team player vibe as they would have to wrok together with other players to defeat the enemies based on their playstyles strengths and weaknesses.
